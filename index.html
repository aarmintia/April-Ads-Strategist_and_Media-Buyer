Case studies

Two independent brand reviews using the frameworks I apply to client work.

Human Nature: what the ads say, and to whom

Framework: 3Ms of Marketing
QuestionAre Human Nature's Meta ads speaking to a clear desire, at the right awareness stage, in a way competitors can't copy?
MethodReviewed the brand's ads against Market Desire, Market Awareness, and Market Sophistication.
Bottom lineStrong, differentiated positioning. Focus is diluted because one message set tries to serve every stage of awareness.

Market desire

The messaging responds to demand for natural personal care and Filipino-made authenticity, tied to wellness and eco-conscious pride.

So what: The desire is real and emotional. It should lead the creative, ahead of product features.

Market awareness

Ads span the range from problem-aware to most aware. That shows broad positioning, but it also risks diluted focus.

So what: A cold audience and a returning buyer need different messages. Mixing them weakens both.

Market sophistication

The brand leans on unique-benefit positioning (locally made, eco-conscious) and sometimes reaches identity-driven storytelling through Filipino pride. This separates it from generic wellness brands.

So what: Identity is the hardest angle for competitors to copy. It's the strongest long-term position.

What I would do next

  1. Give each ad set one awareness stage, with creative written for that stage only.
  2. Make Filipino-pride identity the through-line for cold audiences.
  3. Run a structured creative test on the desire angles and log each hypothesis and result.

CXL: is this business ready to advertise?

Framework: Meta Ads Policy, 7C Compelling Offer, Pixel check
Verdict: ready to advertise and scale. Compliant offer, clear value communication, and functional tracking. Only minor gaps in segmentation and funnel event coverage. Offer score: 27 out of 35.
1. Policy reviewCXL's ads use transparent pricing and focus on professional education. They avoid sensitive claims and exaggerated promises, so they are likely compliant with Meta Ads Policy.
2. Offer evaluationScored against the seven elements of a compelling offer. Details below.
3. Pixel checkTracking is healthy. ViewContent, InitiateCheckout, and PageView are firing.
