01 / Featured case study · FAMA Elite AIP

Class Football Jersey

Where did the buying journey lose momentum, and what would make fans more confident about ordering? As part of FAMA Elite AIP, The Cartalyst group reviewed CFJ’s ads, storefront and customer feedback to prioritize what to check and test.

Week 01 / Audit & growth proposal

The largest event-count gap came before the cart

CFJ sells affordable club, national-team and retro jerseys, with kids’ sets and optional printing on selected items. The client wanted to sell more shirts per order while protecting margins.

Across the selected ads, Meta recorded 19,120 landing page views and 5,629 add-to-cart events. The cart-to-checkout step had the lowest adjacent event ratio at 15.1%. Those gaps gave the team two places to investigate, starting with tracking and a walkthrough of the store on mobile.

Five ads accounted for 420 of 434 purchase events. The storefront review also flagged a mismatch in sale messaging and inconsistent return windows.

Week 02 / Research & insight discovery

Buyers needed clearer answers before ordering

Across research focused on the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany, the team reviewed 163 ads, coded 212 CFJ Trustpilot reviews and profiled five direct rivals. Delivery expectations, fit, return terms and product clarity gave the team concrete ideas to test. The review sample intentionally included every one-star review available to the team at collection.

  • Gift-buying parents needed sizing guidance and confidence the order would arrive in time.
  • Value-conscious fans wanted close-ups and a clear description of what they were buying.
  • Deadline buyers needed a realistic delivery estimate before paying.
Recommendations / Proposed tests

Verify the numbers, then test one change at a time

  1. Check the baseline. Compare Meta purchase events with store orders and walk the mobile path from ad to checkout.
  2. Clarify the offer. Align sale messaging across ads and store. Make confirmed delivery estimates, return terms and sizing easy to find near Add to cart.
  3. Test a trust signal. Compare a benchmark ad with a version that adds the verified review rating. Use purchase cost and order economics to decide whether to expand the test.

The Cartalyst’s group deliverables included an audit and growth proposal, customer and competitor research, and a prioritized testing roadmap. Implementation and post-test results were outside the documented project scope.

Project sources include the CFJ client input form and raw Meta audit pack, The Cartalyst’s Week 1 and Week 2 decks, and the corresponding FAMA Elite AIP project briefs. Meta attribution used 7-day click, 1-day view or 1-day engaged-view.

Methodology

How we work

  1. 01 / Discover

    Define the decision

    Agree on the business goal, the audience and what the team needs to decide.

  2. 02 / Diagnose

    Check the evidence

    Check tracking and read campaign data alongside the ads, storefront and customer feedback. When a survey can answer an open question, decide what to ask before collecting responses.

  3. 03 / Develop

    Brief the test

    Choose a change, explain why it might work and write a brief the team can use.

  4. 04 / Measure

    Review and decide

    Compare test performance with the agreed baseline and success criteria. Report what changed and use the findings to decide what to test next.
