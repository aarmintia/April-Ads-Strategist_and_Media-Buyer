About Me

I am April Armintia, a data-driven marketing enthusiast transitioning into the role of Ads Strategist and Media Buyer. With a foundation in analytics and reporting, I bring precision and structure to campaign planning and optimization.

Through the FAMA Elite Meta Ads Masterclass, I trained in frameworks such as the 3Ms of Marketing (Market Desire, Market Awareness, Market Sophistication) and the FAST Framework for diagnosing ad performance. My portfolio showcases case studies like Human Nature, where I analyzed how brands connect with audiences through eco-conscious and identity-driven messaging.

My goal is to help businesses translate market insights into profitable ad strategies — combining creative positioning with performance metrics such as CTR, ROAS, and CPA. I am passionate about bridging storytelling and data to deliver campaigns that resonate and convert.

Human Nature Case Study

Applying the 3Ms of Marketing framework to Human Nature ads.

Market Desire

In this case study, I analyzed Human Nature’s ads to identify customer desires. The messaging reflects a demand for natural personal care and Filipino-made authenticity, tied to wellness and eco-conscious pride.

Market Awareness

My analysis showed Human Nature’s ads span different awareness stages — from problem-aware to most aware. This demonstrates broad positioning but also highlights the risk of diluted focus.

Market Sophistication

Reviewing Human Nature’s messaging, I found they lean into unique benefit positioning (locally made, eco-conscious) and sometimes reach identity-driven storytelling (Filipino pride). This helps them differentiate from generic wellness brands.
